Follow-up recommendations

Non-diagnostic, evidence-based guidance for the treating clinician.

  • observe

    Continue periodic multimodal monitoring on the current schedule.

  • repeat assessment

    Repeat a clinician-administered cognitive assessment (MMSE/MoCA) to corroborate the digital trend.

  • review reversible

    Review potentially reversible contributors: sleep disturbance, mood, medication effects, metabolic and vascular factors.

  • repeat assessment

    Multimodal discordance detected. Bring the patient in for review before revising any impression.

Potentially reversible contributors

Screen for pseudodementia, anticholinergic load, metabolic deficiency and sleep apnoea before attributing change to neurodegeneration.
